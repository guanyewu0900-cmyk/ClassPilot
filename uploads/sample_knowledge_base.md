# Newton's First Law: sample knowledge base

- An object remains at rest or in uniform straight-line motion when the net external force is zero.
- A nonzero net force changes an object's velocity by changing its speed, direction, or both.
- Friction and air resistance often make everyday moving objects slow down.
- A smoother surface generally produces less friction, allowing an object to travel farther under otherwise similar conditions.
- In an idealized frictionless situation, a moving object does not require a continuing force to maintain uniform straight-line motion.
- During the experiment, ask students to identify variables, record observations, and use their own evidence before drawing a conclusion.
